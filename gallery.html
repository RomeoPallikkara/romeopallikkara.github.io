About this sky

The background is procedurally generated and illustrative, not a data image. Stars sit on the celestial sphere with a Milky Way band built from the galactic-to-equatorial transform, magnitudes follow N ∝ 100.55 m, colours are blackbody temperatures, and there is no twinkling. The nebula uses emission ∝ n² with Hα, [O III] and Hβ colours plus dust that reddens light behind it. The galaxy has an r1/4 bulge, an exponential disc and two logarithmic arms (pitch about 20°, within the observed range for spirals). Deep-field colours use JWST chromatic ordering (F115W/F200W/F444W as blue/green/red) with the Lyα break and intergalactic absorption. Readouts assume flat ΛCDM, H0 = 70 km/s/Mpc, Ωm = 0.3. The cursor trail's colour follows blackbody temperature with pointer speed.
